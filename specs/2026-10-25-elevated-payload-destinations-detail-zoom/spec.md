## 1. Goal, scope, and precedence

Demonstrate clearly visible ascent and descent while the simulated drone transports its suspended package through ten destinations. Raise every destination and increase their vertical spread. Enlarge the drone, suspension, and package in the **right-hand detail panel**, reducing empty space without clipping or distracting camera motion.

This specification explicitly authorizes changing destination **z coordinates** and the right-hand camera. It supersedes the predecessor's restrictions against those two changes. Keep destination x/y, IDs, priorities, event definitions, routing objective, physical assets, attachment geometry, link length, damping, motor authority, load distribution, and settling thresholds unchanged. The left panel remains a fixed overview containing all ten destinations; it may be reframed only to accommodate the new altitude envelope.

Keep MuJoCo and controller-only flight as the primary approach. A finite comparison of the existing Masked PPO routing model against three 3D-fine-tuned candidates is now required under §5.1. Genesis, GPU parallelism demonstrations, flight-NN training, fixed/random-load flight-NN comparisons, and open-ended routing optimization remain outside this change. Flight control and learned route selection must be labeled separately; do not call controller-only flight a learned flight policy.

The numerical altitude transform, camera starting distance, visibility metrics, and finite training/selection constants below are specification-author design choices under the user's request, not already demonstrated performance or separately user-selected numbers. Implement and measure them; do not silently reduce altitude spread or relax success criteria to obtain a video. If a requirement proves incompatible, preserve the failed evidence and propose a concrete specification amendment.

Randomizing all coordinates would unnecessarily change horizontal routing. Use a closer native rendering camera rather than enlarging the panel or magnifying rendered pixels.
